About Our Clinic

Sai Pet Clinic in Mogappair has been a cornerstone of animal healthcare in North Chennai. Under the expert leadership of Dr. Dhananjayan, our clinic provides compassionate, evidence-based veterinary care for dogs, cats, and small animals.
